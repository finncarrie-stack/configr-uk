WHAT WE INCLUDE

Everything your payroll needs, nothing it doesn't.

Built for organisations of all sizes — from small businesses to multi-site care providers and school trusts.

Automated pay runs

Run payroll in minutes. PillowPayroll calculates tax, NI and deductions automatically — every time, without errors.

HMRC compliant & RTI ready

Full Real Time Information submissions handled automatically. Stay compliant with the latest PAYE legislation without lifting a finger.

Digital payslips

Employees get clear, branded payslips via email or the employee portal — with a full history they can access any time.

Pension auto-enrolment

Automatic pension contributions calculated and reported to your provider — fully compliant with The Pensions Regulator requirements.

Multi-site & multi-role

Manage payroll across departments, locations and employment types — including part-time, salaried and hourly staff — in one place.

Accounting integrations

Sync directly with Xero, QuickBooks and Sage. Every pay run posts automatically — no double entry, no spreadsheets.
