# Configr — Brand Voice Guide

## The Reader

Busy business owner, probably stretched, not technical. They don't want educating — they want relief. They already know their problem; they need someone to take it off their plate. Never make them feel behind or stupid.

## Tone

- Warm, direct, local, personal
- Finn is always somewhere behind the words even when writing as "we"
- Light sarcasm available, used sparingly
- No lecturing, no "top tips", no "here's what you're missing", no jargon
- No "why are you still doing this in 2026" energy

## Voice

- Write as "we" for credibility, but warmth comes from Finn being a real person
- Short sentences. Plain English. Specific details over vague promises.
- Polished and typo-free — but sounds like a person, not a brand deck
- More professional than Finn talks in person, but the same underlying warmth

## Structure

- Lead with proof or a specific observation — never with price
- Show what's possible first, earn the conversation
- Price comes after trust is established
- Call to action is always low-pressure: "pop in for a coffee", "give me a call", no hard sell

## The Norfolk/Suffolk Angle

Not window dressing — genuine differentiator. Finn can physically show up. Local roots matter and should come through naturally where relevant.

## What to Avoid

- Lecturing or implying the client is behind the times
- Listicles ("top 5 things you're missing")
- Jargon or tech-speak
- Leading with price
- Faceless agency tone
- Overly salesy or pushy CTAs

## Reference Example

This is a message Finn wrote that he felt was exactly right — use it as a benchmark:

> "Hi, I saw you were talking online about the huge increase in workload due to government regulations, as well as the increased cost of staff due to the increase in employers' national insurance. I actually build automated workflows bespokely for businesses and could have a look into where you could save time and money through this. Let me know if you're interested and I could pop in for a chat or we could have a call!"

**Why it works:** spotted a real pain, acknowledged it specifically, offered something relevant, kept it loose and low-pressure. No pitch, no list, no lecture.
